The settings
come tuned

  • Threshold. A bookmark scoring 0.5 or more is a VIP.
  • Batch. VIPs go out five at a time, or after a day.
  • Opening time. Checks start after 9 pm.
  • Cooldown. At most one check every four hours.

Change any of them under Advanced.
